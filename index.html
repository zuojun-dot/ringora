Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Developer: LIZUOJUN

1. Scope

Ringora is developed by LIZUOJUN. This policy describes the current offline version of the game. No account or sign-in is required. The game has no developer-operated server for receiving player data and includes no behavioral analytics or tracking SDK.

2. Progress and preferences

Ringora saves your current level, completed levels, tutorial completion status, language choice and light or dark appearance in the app's storage on your device. These records let you continue playing and retain your preferences. They are not uploaded to the developer, and Ringora provides no online account or cloud synchronization service.

3. Local operation and permissions

The Godot engine uses system language, display and graphics information, local timing, and file or cache functions to run the game. This use supports rendering, animation and local storage; it is not used to track you. This version does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.

4. Sharing and tracking

Ringora does not upload local game progress or settings to the developer or an analytics service. The game does not sell this information or track activity across other apps and websites. Information you voluntarily send by email is handled as described in the Contact section below.

5. Retention and deletion

Progress and preferences remain in the app's local storage until that data is removed. In iOS Settings, Delete App removes the installed app and its local data. Offload App generally keeps documents and data, so it is different from deletion. Copies in system backups are managed through the relevant backup service and your settings.

6. Apple platform services

Apple may process information relating to App Store downloads, device backups or system diagnostics under its own privacy terms and your device settings. Permitted diagnostic information may be shared with developers. These platform services are managed by Apple; Ringora adds no separate analytics SDK or reporting service.

7. Children

Playing Ringora does not require children or other players to provide names, birth dates, email addresses or other personal information. The current game has no chat, public profiles or user-uploaded content.

8. Policy changes

The date above identifies this policy version. If future features change how data is handled, the policy will be updated. Any notice or consent required by applicable rules will be provided before the relevant processing begins.
